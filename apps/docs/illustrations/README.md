# Voke risograph cut-paper illustrations

> The PNG masters live in this folder (`apps/docs/illustrations/`). The site serves cropped WebP
> files from `apps/docs/public/illustrations/`, built by `python3 scripts/build-illustrations.py`
> (run from `apps/docs`; needs Pillow and `cwebp`).

Final direction D family, produced with the built-in imagegen tool. These masters replace the earlier engraved telecom illustrations.

- `hero.png` — 1536 × 1024 — Nine loose-field sockets, woven ribbons, two loose disc ends, and three lamps; empty left third.
- `functions.png` — 1024 × 1024 — One arriving ribbon seated in one socket, with one lamp.
- `http.png` — 1024 × 1024 — One incoming ribbon branching into three sockets.
- `events.png` — 1024 × 1024 — Five source ribbons converging into one socket.
- `durable.png` — 1024 × 1024 — One loop passing through four checkpoint sockets, with a lamp at the last.
- `local.png` — 1024 × 1024 — A contained socket, disc, and short ribbon inside a rounded bone square.
- `deploy.png` — 1024 × 1024 — A straight vertical ribbon crossing a horizontal bone threshold into a socket, with three lamps in a horizontal row.
- `plugins.png` — 1024 × 1024 — A notched bone circle accepting a rounded blue block.
- `reference.png` — 1024 × 1024 — Three offset rounded index cards crossed by a blue bookmark ribbon, with one lamp.

## Art direction and references

Bold flat cut-paper geometry, broad ribbons, bone rings, solid blue plug discs, tiny amber lamps, coarse paper grain inside shapes, and darker-blue overprint at crossings. No literal machines, text, outlines, or rendered material detail. The approved external `style-options/d-riso.png` was the hero style reference. Both that image and the accepted hero were supplied as style references for every spot. Every image passed on its first generation; no retries were needed.

Approved D reference: `/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-victormesquita--zeron-worktrees-voke-quiet-quartz/13c1e4a5-0b31-45b7-b0a0-500d4335844b/scratchpad/style-options/d-riso.png`.

## Export and verification

The hero is 1536 × 1024; spots are 1024 × 1024. Square tool outputs were resized with Lanczos resampling. Background-only cleanup uses installed Pillow: snap near-black pixels with maximum RGB channel at most 32 and channel spread at most 14 to exact #0C0E12, then smoothly feather the outer 6% to that color. Preserve grain, ink tones, overprint, and antialiasing within artwork; no artwork quantization or posterization.

All final perimeter pixels are verified exact #0C0E12. The hero left third is entirely exact background. Visible artwork bounds (maximum channel above 45) leave at least 12% empty clearance on every side in every image. Amber coverage is below 0.36% of each canvas. Individual visual review and a normalized contact-sheet review found a consistent abstract cut-paper family, without text, literal machines, or object rendering. Ink grain and antialiasing retain generated tonal variation; the artwork is not a strict four-color indexed bitmap. Registration offsets are subtle. No remaining visual concerns.
